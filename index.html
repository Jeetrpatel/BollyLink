    How to play

    1. You start on a 🎬 film and must reach the 🎯 target film (use the 🔍 on the target pill to search).
    2. Pick your game with Play ▾ — 🎲 Random game (random start & target) or 🎬 Pick a starter (choose your start, we give a random target).
    3. Film selected? — its CAST appears below, plus director/writer/producer/music pills in the card. Tap any artist to travel through them.
    4. Artist selected? — their movies appear below. Tap one to jump there; the chain grows film → artist → film.
    5. Connect in as few links as you can — par is the shortest path (BFS on the film–artist graph).
    6. One new puzzle every day, same for everyone. Timer starts when you arrive. ⏱️
